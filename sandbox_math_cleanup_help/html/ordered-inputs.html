Safety boundaries

Array/field receivers may use resolved locals/parameters, lexical fields, unqualified this, type qualifiers and local-rooted nonvolatile field chains. Mutable links and values are reread. Any volatile link, effectful receiver or unsupported computed receiver excludes the shape. Accessor receivers remain simple locals/parameters; static, generic, parameterized and varargs accessors are excluded. Known scalar/SAM/operation summaries take priority over opaque capture.

Exact Byte, Short, Character, Integer and Long values qualify only at resolved Java unboxing or an eligible explicit primitive conversion. The original call/null conversion stays in the capture. Boxed reference identity/outputs, floating wrappers, arbitrary Number and BigDecimal are unsupported.

Indices can contain primitive integral locals, compile-time constant names, literals, casts and total arithmetic. Calls, mutation, division, remainder, nested index reads and runtime field values in indices remain boundaries. Receiver/index recursion and captured occurrence count are bounded at 64. A read depending on a local produced in the region starts a new region. Earlier throwing arithmetic, observable local commits, writes and control flow prevent unsafe capture placement. Relative get(), bulk reads, buffer writes, floating buffers, checked/fallback profiles and helper-body reads remain outside this contract.

Expression-only lambda/branch edits cannot introduce capture statements. Loop motion excludes reads, calls and unboxing. New captures nested in retained helper/SAM arguments are rejected to avoid duplicate execution. These contexts are independently admitted fragments, not arbitrary combinations.

Ordered primitive array stores

A simple store such as out[i] = (u & v) | (~u & w) can now include its total primitive calculation in the verified region. The destination must be a local one-dimensional integral array, with an index independent of locals produced in that region. The store remains at its original position and ends the region. Later reads execute after it, even when source and destination arrays are the same object. Carry values used afterwards remain live.

Whole-file analysis tries established regions first. Deferred assignments and store regions follow established primary windows; richer reads then use the remaining budget before source tasks/contexts and stream/loop passes. Accepted edits cannot overlap and no phase resets the allowance.

Compound stores, computed receivers, effectful indices and potentially throwing arithmetic remain boundaries. No equality of memory locations or whole-loop invariant is assumed, and repeated reads are not merged. This supports arithmetic feeding individual limb stores, not a general rewrite of multiword algorithms.

Costs and verification

The existing capture cost assessment charges capture stores, references and conservative peak storage. The unchanged original loads and indices are not counted as savings. Readability may accept a clearer expression which is not a runtime win; runtime and allocation goals keep their existing cost gates. Legacy report names ArrayRead and arrayReads identify this same ordered-capture obligation, including the added field/buffer forms.

JUnit coverage uses resolved JDT bindings, actual extraction and numerical proof, exact source edits and Undo, complete-class compilation, and execution comparisons including null/bounds precedence, repeated/dead reads and buffer state. Whole-project hit counts and measured speedups remain separate evidence; source admission alone does not establish either.
